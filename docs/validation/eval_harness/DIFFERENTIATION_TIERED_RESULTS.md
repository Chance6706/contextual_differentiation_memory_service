# Differentiation — the TIERED exploration: STRUCTURAL NEGATIVE (single-pass consolidation) + pivot to DRIFT

## VERDICT: **STRUCTURAL NEGATIVE — at single-pass consolidation, disposition individuation is NOT in the surviving memory state.** The non-degenerate survivor set carries **stochastic per-topic recency, not disposition-structured** individuation, and **salience is mechanistically inert** at the forgetting boundary. Reported honestly; the individuation question **pivots to the DRIFT (recall-reconsolidation) mechanism.**

**Scope disclaimer (load-bearing):** this negative is a property of the **single-pass consolidation** forgetting
mechanism (one consolidate+decay pass per cycle, no recall-time reconsolidation). It does **not** preclude a
mechanism with cross-topic coupling — which is exactly the pivot (§Pivot).

**How it resolved:** the tiered exploration (`DIFFERENTIATION_TIERED_PREREG.md`, EXPLORATORY, DRAFT) was
empirically pre-tested **during** its rule-12 double pressure-test (3 agents). The empirical probe was decisive
**before lock**, so the structural arm was never locked or run as a full study — the pressure-test itself
produced the negative. Nothing here was a confirmatory run; it is the honest exploratory outcome.

## The decisive number (PT2-nondeg, empirical; fastembed, 500 cyc, 14 seeds, q=0.006≈q*, frequency⊥goalset)

- **Non-degeneracy IS achievable:** vary re-live probability `q` (uniform goal) → clean survival sigmoid,
  marginal band at q∈[0.002,0.02], P≈0.5 at the **mechanism-derived** `q*≈1/idle_survival≈1/164≈0.006`.
  Survivor varies seed-to-seed (**14/14 distinct**); `history_effect≈0.65 ≫ 0` → the endpoint tautology is
  genuinely broken.
- **BUT the signal is STOCHASTIC, not disposition-structured (the crux):** HIGH-tier `history_effect = 0.651`
  vs its **seed-shuffle null = 0.633, 95% CI [0.601, 0.658]** — observed sits **INSIDE** the null. The survivor
  variance is fully explained by **independent per-topic recency coin-flips**; there is **no seed-coherent,
  cross-topic structure to beat.** In the prereg's north-star terms: **the interaction does NOT live in the
  structural survivor set.**
- **Salience is INERT in the band (second, independent failure):** goal-gap DiD (salience − random) =
  **−0.036 ≈ 0**. Salience's only channel (retention → support → idle-survival) is live only at *short* re-live
  gaps (<~35 cyc); the marginal band forms at *long* gaps (~167 cyc) where old episodes are gone regardless of
  goal. **The band-forming regime and the salience-sensitive regime do not overlap** → the "salience ≠ random"
  precondition HALTs. Salience/disposition cannot shape *which* topics survive here.

**Why (mechanism):** survival is **per-topic independent** (each topic its own recency coin-flip), so there is
no cross-topic coherence for a disposition to shape; and salience's support-channel is decoupled from the
band-forming regime. A recency readout, not an identity.

## PIVOT — the DRIFT (recall-reconsolidation) mechanism

The negative diagnoses its own fix: disposition-structured individuation requires **cross-topic coupling**,
which single-pass consolidation lacks (survival is per-topic independent). The candidate mechanism is
**bounded recall-drift** ([[project-cdms-recall-reconsolidation]]): recalling memories under the current
disposition drifts them **together** toward that disposition — creating exactly the seed-coherent, cross-topic
structure that was absent.

**The hypothesis (falsifiable):** with bounded recall-drift ON, the goal_hint-fixed `history_effect`
**BEATS the seed-shuffle null** (disposition-coherent structure appears in the survivor); with drift OFF it
sits AT the null (this result). **The seed-shuffle null built here is the ready-made decisive instrument** —
the pressure-test that killed the forgetting-only arm also built the exact test the drift arm needs.

**Arc (next focused pass):** (1) design the bounded recall-drift (extend the support-weighted valence-EMA to
the recall path, current context as evidence; provenance-gated, drifts emphasis/valence NOT facts,
magnitude-capped); (2) its OWN safety review (recall-drift is a confabulation/poisoning surface); (3) build;
(4) run the structural exploration drift-ON vs drift-OFF; (5) adjudicate with the seed-shuffle null.

The language / MSA-A′ arm is **de-prioritized / parked** (Josh: more excited by drift). If revisited, the
shortlist is grammatical **evidentials** (Turkish binary `-mış`/`-dı`, Korean/Japanese), cloud-assisted
screening + native-human confirmation (a native-Korean contact is a possible but schedule-uncertain resource).
